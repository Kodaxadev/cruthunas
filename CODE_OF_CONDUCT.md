# Code of Conduct

Cruthúnas discussions must remain specific, evidence-focused, and respectful. Strong criticism of policies, implementations, assumptions, and AI-assisted workflows is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, ridicule, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not.

Participants should identify exact invariants, files, commands, schemas, or examples when raising a concern; disclose uncertainty and material automated-tool assistance; and distinguish disagreement with a design from judgment of its author.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces controlled by the maintainers. Maintainers may remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct interferes with technical review or community safety.
