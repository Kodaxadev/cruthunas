# Literature

Verified prior sources cited by this project. Every citation used in a manuscript must be checked against its primary source (Gate 8), not just against a secondary summary.

## Rules

- No novelty or attribution claim based solely on secondary summaries.
- Every entry links to (or names precisely) the primary source, not a blog post or AI-generated summary about it.
- If a claim depends on a citation, the dependency is also recorded in `claims/claims.yaml`.
