# AI Use Disclosure

This repository framework assumes AI-assisted research and engineering will be used, and requires that use to be disclosed honestly rather than hidden or overstated.

## Principles

- AI systems are tools, never authors. They are disclosed here and in `CONTRIBUTIONS.md`, not credited under CRediT author roles.
- A fresh AI model context reviewing a proof is internal AI-assisted review. It is never labeled external mathematical review, adversarial peer review, or a substitute for a named human reviewer.
- "Formalized in a proof assistant" is not equivalent to "peer-reviewed." "CI passed" is not equivalent to "mathematically reviewed." These distinctions are enforced by the three-axis status system in `RESEARCH_CHARTER.md`.
- Any manuscript produced under this framework must include an AI-use disclosure section (Gate 8) describing which stages used AI assistance (exploration, drafting, proof search, formalization support, editing) and which did not.
- Author responsibility for correctness remains with the named human author(s) regardless of tooling used.
